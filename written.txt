Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?
The bounding boxes grow and overlap more as the circles move because refitting updates the boxes
but keeps the original tree structure and circle groupings.

Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?
The bounding boxes stay tighter around nearby circles because reset rebuilds the tree using the
circles' new positions every frame.

Q5. Why are some circles drawn with their sides cut off?
The circles move before the pixels are drawn, but reset rebuilds the bounding boxes after the
pixels are drawn. The old boxes can reject points on the moved circles' edges.
